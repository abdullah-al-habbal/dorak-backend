# 03 — Website Persona Journeys
> These stories focus strictly on the visitor's experience on the **public website**, not the app.

## Story A: Khaled (The Brand Owner)
Khaled lands on the Dorak website. He sees a sleek, dark-slate design with subtle gold accents. He doesn't see "pink" or "blue" — he sees **premium business software**. He scrolls to the "Interactive Floor Plan" section, taps a demo chair, and sees how easy booking is. He clicks "Pricing," sees that his first branch is free, and clicks "Create Brand," which redirects him to the Filament Admin panel.

## Story B: Layla (The Client)
Layla visits the site to see what Dorak is. She clicks the "Women's Beauty" toggle at the top. The site's accent colors smoothly shift to an elegant rose gold, and the hero imagery changes to high-end salon interiors. She watches a 10-second animation of the floor plan, clicks "Download App," and gets the Flutter app. The core layout never changed, but the *vibe* perfectly matched her universe.

## Story C: Rasha (The Freelance Barber)
Rasha lands on the site. She navigates to the "Features" page and reads about the "Standalone Barber" invariant. She sees that she can own her profile, offer at-home services, and affiliate with shops without losing her data. She feels empowered and clicks the link to download the Barber app.
